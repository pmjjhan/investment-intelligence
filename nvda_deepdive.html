04 Core Thesis — Bull Case 4 Pillars
CUDA Moat + Switching Cost Compounding
Consensus
"10+ years of CUDA ecosystem investment creates a developer moat that AMD ROCm and Intel XPU cannot close within a single capex cycle."
  • BVP estimates 4.5M+ CUDA-trained ML engineers globally; retraining cost per org runs $2-8M for mid-size model teams, making switching economically irrational absent a 40%+ cost delta [BVP Atlas]
  • Sequoia's Sonya Huang notes all major foundation model labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI) have H200/B200 clusters locked in through 2027 - no hedging visible at workload level [Sequoia]
  • Goldman Sachs infrastructure survey: 91% of enterprise AI buyers cite CUDA compatibility as non-negotiable in procurement - highest barrier-to-entry score in 15 years of hardware surveys [Goldman]
Inference-Time Compute Scaling Extends the Upgrade Cycle
Emerging
"Shift from pre-training scaling to test-time compute (chain-of-thought, multi-agent reasoning) creates a second demand wave that wasn't priced into original AI infrastructure thesis."
  • Not Boring analysis: inference-optimized workloads now represent 55% of new GPU cluster deployments vs. 22% in 2024 - directionally validating a multi-year demand extension [Not Boring]
  • Coatue's public deck (Q1 2026) explicitly models inference scaling as "pre-training TAM × 3-5x" - the single largest revision to their AI infrastructure sizing since 2023 [Coatue]
  • Benedict Evans: "Every AI product team I talk to is GPU-constrained on inference, not training - this is the structural shift nobody modeled" [@benedictevans]
Sovereign AI and Geographic Demand Diversification
Emerging
"Nation-state AI buildouts (EU, India, Middle East, Japan) represent a second demand cohort structurally decoupled from US hyperscaler capex - providing cycle independence that prior semiconductor upcycles never had."
  • Goldman Top of Mind (May 2026): sovereign AI spending running at $42B annualized globally ex-China/US, with India ($8B), UAE ($6B), and France ($5B) as lead buyers - all predominantly Nvidia-architected [Goldman]
  • a16z infrastructure brief: sovereign clusters uniquely require full-stack Nvidia (DGX Cloud + networking + software) due to security certification requirements, creating higher ASP per deal than hyperscaler relationships [a16z]
  • Tomasz Tunguz: "Sovereign AI is the most underappreciated demand driver in every model I've seen - analyst consensus is missing 15-20% of Nvidia's 2027 revenue" [Tunguz]
NIM + Networking Stack: Platform Margin Expansion
Early
"Nvidia's software layer (NIM microservices, NEMO, DGX Cloud) and InfiniBand networking are converting a hardware company into a platform with 80%+ gross margin software revenue layered on top of chip sales."

Algorithmic Efficiency Renders Current GPU Generation Obsolete Faster
Monitoring
  • Deepseek R2 + similar efficiency-first architectures demonstrated 10x inference cost reductions without hardware upgrades - compresses the economic case for B200 over H100 at current price delta [Not Boring]
  • a16z research: model efficiency doubling every 8 months (faster than Moore's Law equivalent) - if this holds, 2026 GPU vintage faces accelerated obsolescence risk vs. historical 3-4 year amortization [a16z]
  • Benedict Evans: "The labs are spending less per FLOP every 6 months - the question is whether that reduces capex or enables more ambition. History says more ambition, but this is not certain." [@benedictevans]
Thesis Break Threshold
If model FLOP efficiency improves >5x within 18 months AND hyperscaler workload growth doesn't absorb the efficiency gain (i.e., flat or declining GPU hours purchased), Jevons Paradox fails to hold.
China Export Restrictions Permanently Cap Addressable Market
Critical
  • Current BIS licensing regime blocks H20, A800, and all future advanced chips to China - represents estimated $15-20B annual revenue forfeiture at peak; Nvidia guided $5.5B inventory charge in prior quarter [Goldman]
  • China domestic GPU ecosystem (Biren, Cambricon, Huawei Ascend) receiving $40B+ in state subsidies through 2028 - long-horizon threat to recapture domestic demand that Nvidia cannot serve [Morgan Stanley]
  • Turner Novak: "Everyone's underwriting the China restriction as temporary - I'd argue it's permanent and the TAM math hasn't caught up yet" [@TurnerNovak]
Thesis Break Threshold
If US-China technology decoupling accelerates to include allied nation restrictions (Japan, Netherlands ASML export controls tightened) OR China domestic GPU achieves >70% workload compatibility with CUDA, thesis on sovereign AI demand diversification is partially offset.

02
How durable is NIM pricing power once open-source inference frameworks (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM) achieve enterprise-grade SLAs?
vLLM and TensorRT-LLM open-source are closing the gap on enterprise reliability benchmarks. If the managed service premium for NIM compresses from ~40% to ~15% within 24 months (similar to what happened to Docker Enterprise vs. Kubernetes), the software margin expansion thesis is impaired. No source analyzed this transition risk with sufficient specificity.
Monitoring · Watch: vLLM enterprise adoption curve, NIM pricing disclosures at GTC
03
At what point does China's Huawei Ascend + domestic GPU ecosystem achieve CUDA partial compatibility via emulation or translation layers?
China's domestic AI hardware ecosystem is receiving $40B+ in state subsidies. The critical unknown is not raw chip performance (Ascend 910C is reportedly competitive with H100 on some benchmarks) but software compatibility. If a CUDA translation layer achieves >80% workload coverage, China-based AI companies can potentially switch - and the precedent would pressure global customers. No credible technical analysis of this pathway found in available sources.
Monitoring · Watch: Huawei developer conference announcements, Chinese academic papers on CUDA compatibility
04
Does the Blackwell Ultra (B300) architecture cycle generate a forced upgrade inflection, or do H200 clusters remain "good enough" for 2026-2027 inference workloads?
The bull case for continued revenue growth requires customers to upgrade from H200 to B200/B300 on a 12-18 month cycle. But inference workloads, unlike training, are less sensitive to peak FLOP improvements and more sensitive to memory bandwidth and cost per token. If B300 doesn't deliver >2x memory bandwidth improvement over H200 for inference use cases, upgrade cycle extends and near-term revenue growth slows. Available sources don't have confirmed B300 inference benchmark comparisons.
Monitoring · Watch: B300 technical disclosures at Hot Chips 2026, hyperscaler procurement guidance
