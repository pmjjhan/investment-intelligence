9. Falsification Panel - What Would Have to Be True for This to Be Wrong
1. Microsoft Copilot out-executes at the workflow layer. If Microsoft bundles AI agent workflows into M365 at no incremental cost, NOW's monetization of AI workflows is at risk. The bear case is that enterprise IT just pays more to Microsoft and routes around NOW's CMDB as the source of truth for agent actions.
2. AI agent adoption stalls at pilot stage. If enterprise AI agent deployment stays in POC/pilot mode for 18+ months due to compliance, liability, or integration complexity, NOW's AIOps and Pro Plus SKU ramp doesn't materialize on the revenue line in the expected FY26-27 timeframe.
3. Multiple compression from macro or rate environment. At $114, NOW still trades at 25x+ forward FCF. If rates remain elevated and growth multiples compress further - even with strong fundamentals - the stock could stay flat or decline despite good business performance. This is a valuation risk, not a business risk.
