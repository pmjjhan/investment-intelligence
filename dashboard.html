EARLY SIGNAL The Harness Layer as Durable Moat
Tomasz Tunguz ▼

As frontier models commoditize, the competitive moat migrates to whoever controls the seven-layer AI harness: context + memory, tools, orchestration, state persistence, sandbox, observability, and cost optimization. When all teams access identical frontier models, "the best riders win" — differentiator shifts from model capability to orchestration quality. Inference is the largest and fastest-growing market in tech ($250B projected in 7 years), but inference itself commoditizes. The harness does not.
